who we are

Everyone's always busy. Between school, sports, jobs, and our phones, we barely stop to check in with ourselves, and we don't really talk to the people around us anymore. We noticed it in our own lives first, and then we started seeing it everywhere, even in Prospect Park, where tons of people show up every weekend but hardly anyone talks to each other.

So we started Grounded. We're a bunch of teenagers from Brooklyn. We're not therapists or experts. We just care a lot about mental health and community and wanted to actually do something about it. Every other week we meet up in the park for guided meditation, yoga, tai chi, potlucks, and jam sessions.

If it feels like everything's been moving too fast, come hang out with us for thirty minutes. Leave your phone in your bag, meet some new people, and give yourself a minute to breathe. You don't need any experience. You just need to show up.
